## Commentary

March year-to-date insurance expense was initially EUR 120,000, EUR 90,000
above the EUR 30,000 expected run rate, because the full annual premium was
expensed on January 1. The policy confirms that EUR 90,000 relates to April–
December coverage. Finance reclassified that amount to prepaid insurance,
reducing YTD expense to EUR 30,000 and eliminating the apparent overspend.

This is a one-off classification correction, not evidence of recurring cost
pressure. Finance will add an annual-prepayment review to the monthly close
checklist and retain the policy and amortization schedule with the journal.
Owner: Finance Manager. Expected April effect: EUR 10,000 insurance expense
through the monthly amortization schedule.

## Decision requested

Approve the control change and continue the EUR 10,000 monthly amortization
from April through December. No operating-cost reduction action is required
for this variance.
