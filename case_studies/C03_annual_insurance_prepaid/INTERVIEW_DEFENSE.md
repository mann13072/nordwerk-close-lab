# Interview defense — C03

### Why is this a prepaid expense?

The policy documents a specified service period after March 31. The nine
unconsumed months represent a future-period benefit, so the amount is not
current-period expense at the reporting date.

### Why did you not reverse the journal on April 1?

This is a reclassification supported by a continuing schedule, not a temporary
service accrual. The balance is released through nine monthly EUR 10,000
amortization entries.

### What evidence supports the adjustment?

The policy supports the coverage period, the bank statement supports payment,
the ledger supports the original posting, and the schedule supports the EUR
90,000 calculation. The adjustment should not be approved without the policy.

### Is this an HGB statutory close?

No. It is a simulated March management close using HGB-based recognition and
presentation concepts. It is not an audited annual financial statement.

### Did you use SAP?

No. The underlying portfolio uses ERP-shaped synthetic exports. No live SAP
instance or proprietary data was used.

### What changed in management reporting?

YTD insurance expense fell from EUR 120,000 to EUR 30,000, matching the three
months consumed. The apparent EUR 90,000 overspend disappeared.

### What remains human judgment?

A reviewer must confirm that the contract is genuine, the coverage period is
complete, the payment belongs to the entity, and there are no cancellation,
refund, or impairment facts that change the conclusion.
